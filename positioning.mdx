## Positioning

**The standing real estate partner for the multi-property investor.**

JFC Immobilier is the one accountable partner an investor keeps across the whole life of a property: buying, short-term and long-term management, and selling, with the advice to adapt the portfolio as the rules change. Conciergeries only manage. Agencies only sell. Chasseurs disappear after the deal. JFC stays.

## Internal north star

> They sell, or they manage, or they hunt, and then they are gone. JFC does all three and stays.

Use this to judge whether a piece of content belongs to JFC. **It is not a slogan to repeat in public.** If a draft never points back to this idea, it is building someone else's brand.

## Brand personality

**An expert who makes complicated things simple, never talks down, and is honest about risk.**

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Is" icon="circle-check">
    Experienced, direct, numerate, unhurried. Explains the trade-off before the upside. Answers the question that was actually asked.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Is not" icon="circle-x">
    The salesperson. The hype account. The anonymous corporate voice. The advisor who only shows the good scenario.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Tip>
  **The accountability rule.** A named, accountable founder is the one thing competitors cannot copy. Content that hides Jérôme behind a faceless company voice throws that away for nothing.
</Tip>

## What JFC is not

Useful when a content idea feels close but wrong.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Not a conciergerie">
    Management is one of three lines, not the business. Content that reduces JFC to cleaning and check-ins undersells the advisory.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Not a listings portal">
    Nobody chooses JFC for inventory. They choose it for judgement about which property to buy and when to sell.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Not a tax adviser">
    JFC explains the tax position in plain terms and works alongside the client's accountant. It does not replace one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
