## What the French pass involves

Not a translation job. A rewrite against the same rules.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Website copy first">
    Highest leverage and the only place needing full EN/FR parity. [Website copy](/website).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Then the post library">
    Fourteen blocks. These are what actually get published week to week. [Post library](/posts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Then the sequences">
    Nine drafted emails plus B1 and B4, which are still to write in either language. [Email sequences](/emails).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Boilerplate and buttons last">
    Short, high-visibility, and easiest to get wrong. [Brand platform](/positioning), [Visual identity](/visual).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Keep sentence length. French business writing defaults longer and more formal than this voice wants. If the FR version reads more formal than the EN version, the pass has failed.
</Note>
