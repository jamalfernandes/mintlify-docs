## Headline formulas

Five shapes that fit the voice. Most posts and email subject lines should be one of these.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The number, then the consequence">
    "Fifteen apartments under management. Not one owner has handled a check-in."
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The comparison">
    "Most owners work with three companies. Here is what that costs them."
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The rule explained">
    "What the registration requirement actually means if you own two flats."
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The real question a client asked">
    "A client asked whether to sell or keep renting. Here is how I answered."
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The honest trade-off">
    "Short-term earns more. It also costs more, in three specific ways."
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  **Avoid** anything that starts with a rhetorical question about whether you are making the most of your investment. That is the sound of every other agency.
</Note>

<Tip>
  **The proof rule.** Specifics instead of adjectives, every time. A claim with no number, name or source attached is filler. This applies to profile bios and website headings as much as to posts.
</Tip>
