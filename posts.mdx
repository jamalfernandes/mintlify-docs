### 03 · The returns method

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="EN">
    ```text The returns method · EN
    We target a minimum seven percent return. Here is what sits behind that number, because a target you cannot defend is worth nothing.
    
    We calculate on the full cost, not the purchase price. That means notary fees, the renovation, the furniture, and the months the flat earns nothing while the work happens.
    
    We use realistic occupancy, not peak season. Cleaning, platform fees and management come out before the number, not after.
    
    Some properties do not clear seven percent. We say so, and we tell you why before you commit rather than after.
    
    The brochure number and the real number are rarely the same. Ask for the working.
    ```
