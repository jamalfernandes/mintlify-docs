Every account gets one job. The June audit found four accounts each saying something slightly different to a slightly different person, which is effort that never compounds. This is the fix.

## One job per account

| Channel | Audience | Job | Register | Themes | Weekly |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Corporate LinkedIn | ICP 1 | Authority and inbound | Premium, precise | Regulation explainers, investor case studies, portfolio thinking, LMNP and tax | 2 posts |
| Personal LinkedIn | ICP 1 | Trust and reach | Founder, direct | Behind the business, lessons, milestones, market opinion | 1 to 2 posts |
| Short-term Instagram | ICP 2 | Lead capture | Warm, visual | Property storytelling, guest experience, management reassurance, compliance made simple | 2 posts, 3 stories |
| Personal Instagram | Both | Human proof | Light, personal | Lifestyle and personality | Ad hoc |
| Airbnb co-host, Le Bon Coin | ICP 2 | Capture and credibility | Warm, factual | Kept current and worded from this kit | Monthly check |
| Website | Both | Credibility close | Premium, precise | Proof, clarity, one low-friction lead magnet | Quarterly |
| Email | Both | Convert and reactivate | Matches the audience | Three sequences, see [Email sequences](/emails) | Automated |

<Note>
  The website is **not a discovery channel** at around 50 visits a month. People arrive having already heard the name. The site's only job is to confirm JFC is real.
</Note>

## The weekly rhythm

Four posts a week, batched in one session. The point of batching is that consistency stops depending on how busy Jérôme's week was.

| Day | What goes out |
| --- | --- |
| **Mon** | Corporate LinkedIn. Rule or market explainer. |
| **Tue** | Instagram. Property or guest story. |
| **Wed** | Personal LinkedIn. Client situation or opinion. |
| **Thu** | Email. Sequences send on their own schedule. |
| **Fri** | Corporate LinkedIn. Case study or proof point. |
| **Sat** | Instagram. Lighter, visual. |
| **Sun** | Off. Batch and approve next week. |

## Content mix

Share of posts by theme across a month. Regulation leads because it is the strongest engine JFC has: it is genuinely useful, it repeats as the rules change, and almost nobody else explains it plainly.

| Theme | Share |
| --- | --- |
| Rules and regulation | 30% |
| Client stories | 25% |
| Returns and portfolio | 20% |
| Behind the business | 15% |
| Property and guests | 10% |

Roughly **three quarters of output speaks to the investor**, because that is where the revenue is. The remaining quarter keeps the owner channel alive and feeds the investor pool over time.

## Language by channel

JFC publishes in French and English. The split is not even, and it is not a translation exercise.

| Channel | Default | English when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Corporate LinkedIn | French | The post is about investing from abroad, or the case study involves a non-resident owner. |
| Personal LinkedIn | French | Rarely. This is Jérôme's own voice and his network is French. |
| Short-term Instagram | French | Captions can carry a short English second paragraph where the property appeals to international guests. |
| Website | Both | Full parity. See [Website copy](/website). |
| Email | Routed | Sequence language follows the contact's language, captured at signup. |
| Lead magnet | Both | The guide itself needs both versions before either sequence can send. |
