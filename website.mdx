The site is a **credibility close, not a discovery channel**. At around 50 visits a month, nobody is finding JFC here. They arrive having already heard the name, and they are checking whether it is real. The copy is written for that job.

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Job" icon="target">
    Confirm JFC is a real operator with real numbers, then capture the visitor who is not ready to call.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Success" icon="circle-check">
    A call booked, or the guide downloaded. Two actions, nothing else competing.
  </Card>

  <Card title="From the audit" icon="gauge">
    Mobile speed held back by a 4.2 second largest paint. Title and meta tags overlong. No business schema.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Note>
  The website is the one place where **French and English need full parity**. A non-resident investor checking credibility will not persevere through a French-only page. Both languages ship together or the English version does not ship.
</Note>

## Lead magnet landing page

### Wireframe

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/wireframe-lead-magnet.svg" alt="Wireframe of the lead magnet landing page, two columns" />
</Frame>

**Three fields, one button, nothing else.** Every additional field costs conversions, and the third field is the one that earns its place: it routes the download into the right sequence.

### Copy

  <Tab title="EN">

    What registration actually requires, and where the number has to appear.

<Tip>
  **The property-count field is the one that matters.** It routes the download into the investor sequence or the owner sequence, which is what makes [Email sequences](/emails) work. Add a language field alongside it so the sequence sends in the right language.
</Tip>
